ACTIVITY 4: ONOS INTENT FRAMEWORK

1. Start the Diamond topology:

sudo mn --custom ~/diamond.py --topo diamond \
--controller remote,ip=127.0.0.1,port=6653 \
--switch ovsk,protocols=OpenFlow13

2. Check available devices:

devices

3. Check discovered hosts:

hosts

4. Check network links:

links

5. Create a bandwidth-constrained host-to-host intent:

add-host-intent -b 10 00:00:00:00:00:01/None 00:00:00:00:00:02/None

6. Verify intent status:

intents

7. Inspect installed intent paths and flow rules:

intents -i

8. Test connectivity:

h1 ping -c 5 10.0.0.2

9. Simulate failure of the active path (Mininet CLI):

link s1 s2 down

10. Verify the remaining links and intent state:

links
intents
intents -i

11. Test connectivity after rerouting:

h1 ping -c 10 10.0.0.2

EXPECTED RESULTS:

- Intent 0xa reached INSTALLED state.
- Initial path: S1 -> S2 -> S4.
- After the S1-S2 link failure, ONOS rerouted traffic through S1 -> S3 -> S4.
- Final connectivity test: 10 packets transmitted, 10 received, 0% packet loss.
